title: Robot War: Merge Defense — Privacy Policy
---


Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Robot War: Merge Defense game ("the game"), made by Roshan Gunarathna ("we"), collects and how it is used.

## Information the game stores on your device

Your game progress (soldiers, coins, upgrades, best wave and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

## In-app purchases

If you buy something in the game, the payment is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, not your payment details. See <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

## Ratings and feedback

After some boss waves the game may ask Google Play (or the App Store) to show its rating window. Your rating goes to the store, not to us.

If you use "Send feedback" in the pause menu, your message, any screenshot you choose to attach (you can draw on it before sending) and your email address, if you choose to give it, are sent to us through Wiredash, along with the wave you are on, your best wave, the app version and basic device information (model, operating system, language, screen size). The Wiredash software in the game also creates a random installation ID and reports the game's first launch, without your name or contact details. Wiredash stores this data in the European Union. See <https://docs.wiredash.com/company/privacy>. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve the game, and you can ask us to delete a report at any time.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Security

Data sent by the services above is encrypted in transit.

## Your choices

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google, RevenueCat or Wiredash, use the links above. For questions or deletion requests, contact us.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
